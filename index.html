Click a piece to see its powers.

The twist: a piece that captures absorbs every power of its victim and can move like all of them.

Checkmate wins as usual. Pawns that reach the far side become the piece you choose. No castling or en passant.

Kings' feast: if the game would end in a draw because only kings are left, or a player is stuck without being in check, any king that has eaten pieces shakes and spits them all back out as its own pieces.

Pawn eats knight? It can now hop like a knight. Eaten pieces fuse onto the eater in their own colors:

Fog of war is ON: smoke hides every square your team hasn't stood on or passed through, for you and your opponent alike. A piece that runs into a hidden enemy stops and captures it. Haze swirling at a piece's feet means the other side can't see it.

Mystery boxes: every few moves a crate falls. Balloon crates drift down over 3 moves (watch the shadow); plain crates crash down at once and crush whatever is there, except kings. Finish a move on a crate to open it: teleport to a random square, take an extra turn, change into a different piece, or become a secret jester (only you see its hat; whoever eats it dies).

Devour is OFF for this game: captures work like normal chess.
