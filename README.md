# Reckless Driver

A pixelated, top-down arcade game where you're a reckless driver weaving through highway traffic to get ahead of the other cars. Your car starts out **white**. Beat high scores to unlock new paint colors.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. You don't need to build or install anything.

## Controls

| Action | Keys |
| --- | --- |
| Steer | ← → or A / D |
| Gas | ↑ or W |
| Brake | ↓ or S |
| Start / retry | Enter or Space |
| Pause | P or Esc |
| Mute | M |

On phones and tablets, on-screen buttons appear, and you tap the screen to start.

## How to score

- You earn points for distance. Above 120 MPH you're driving **RECKLESS** and earn double points.
- Squeeze past a car with only a few pixels to spare to get a **CLOSE!** bonus. Close calls in a row build a combo.
- Traffic gets heavier the farther you go, and cars signal before they change lanes. Watch for the blinkers!
- Driving on the grass slows you down.
- One crash and you're wrecked.

## Unlockable paint

White → Red (1,500) → Blue (4,000) → Yellow (8,000) → Green (15,000) → Black (25,000) → Hot Pink (40,000)

Your best score and chosen paint are saved in your browser.
