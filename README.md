# Game
For my game

Open `index.html` in a browser to pick a game. No install needed.

## Dungeon Boss (`dungeon.html`)
A reverse dungeon crawler: you are the boss, and a hero is trying to reach your lair.

- **Build:** spend coins on spike traps (click a trap again to upgrade it, up to level 3) and souls on skeletons and ogres.
- **Fight:** if the hero reaches your lair, move with WASD / arrows and swing with Space.
- Every time the hero dies you get coins and souls, but he comes back at a higher level, with better gear and a better chance to dodge.

## Pixel Snake (`snake.html`)
Classic Snake. Arrows / WASD to move, Space to pause, swipe on mobile.
