# Sky Courier

A pixelated top-down canyon run in the same style as Reckless Driver. Fly a courier jet up a winding canyon, grab packages hanging from balloons, drop them on the delivery pads, and dodge the missiles.

Open `index.html` in a browser. It's a single self-contained file.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Touch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Steer | Left/Right or A/D | ◀ ▶ |
| Throttle up | Up or W | GAS |
| Slow down | Down or S | BRK |
| Barrel roll | B | B |
| Start / retry | Enter or Space | Tap the screen |
| Pause | P or Esc | |
| Mute | M | |

## How it works

- **Packages:** fly into a balloon crate to pick it up (**+50**). You can carry 5; the hold is shown as **BOX** at the top right.
- **Drop zones:** green and white targets on the canyon floor. Fly over one to drop your whole load. The payout is **100 × load × load**, so 1 package pays 100 and 5 pay 2,500. Dropping a full load of 5 also repairs 1 HP.
- **Missiles:** turrets on the cliff edges fire homing missiles. From the second stage on, cruise missiles also come straight down at you; a red **!** at the top of the screen shows where.
- **Close calls:** let a missile pass close by for **CLOSE! +50**, multiplied by your combo. Dodging one mid-roll doubles it (**ROLL DODGE!**).
- **Barrel roll (B):** a quick sideways hop that missiles pass straight through. It has a 3-second cooldown, shown by the bar under **B ROLL**.
- **HP:** you have 3. A missile hit or scraping the canyon wall costs 1 and knocks a package out of your hold. At 0 you go down.
- **Afterburn:** above 174 knots (the orange tick on the throttle bar) distance points are doubled.
- **Stages:** about every half minute the canyon gets narrower and the turrets fire more.
