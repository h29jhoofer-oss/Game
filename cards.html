HOW TO PLAY

Everyone plays against the dealer, not each other. Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over.

Number cards count their number, J Q K count 10, and an ace counts 1 or 11, whichever helps.

HIT takes a card. STAND keeps your hand. DOUBLE doubles your bet on your first two cards and takes exactly one more card.

SPLIT turns two cards of the same value into two hands, each with its own bet (up to 4 hands). Split aces get one card each, and a split hand of 21 isn't blackjack.

An ace plus a 10-value card is blackjack and pays 3 to 2. A win pays 1 to 1 and a tie (push) gives your bet back.

Cheater mode (3 to 6 players): one player is the dealer and holds a pistol. Secretly, one player is the cheater (once the hands are dealt, sees the next 3 cards and the dealer's hidden card; wins at $1,000), and anyone else is an honest regular. With 4 or more, one is also the jester (on their turn, sees the card they'd get; wins if shot) and one the gambler (one ace in their pocket to swap into their hand once a game). You only see your own hand total, and your secrets stay hidden until you hold B, so nobody can read your role off your screen. Once a game, any player can call out the gambler: guess right and you take half their money; guess wrong and they take half of yours. The dealer plays the house hand themselves: hit, stand, double (one card, and every bet counts twice) or split a pair (each player's hand plays half its bet against each house hand). The dealer gets one shot: hit the cheater and the house, gambler and regulars win; hit the jester and the jester wins; hit anyone else and the cheater wins.

Bribes mode (3 to 6 players): one player is the dealer. Secretly, there may be 0, 1 or 2 undercover inspectors (nobody is told how many); everyone else is a high roller with a secret motive: greedy (best score after 12 rounds), spiteful (wants everyone else broke), the jester (wants to get caught), the snitch (sends the inspectors anonymous letters) or undercover (bribes with marked chips to get the dealer caught). Click your own chips to slip the dealer a bribe for a favour: PEEK at the hidden card, SWAP a card, a SHIELD for your next loss, or a JINX on someone else's next hand. Each pocketed bribe is also a favour point ($50 at the end). Bribing and pocketing are timing games: fumble and the table hears it, but not who. Inspectors win with 2 catches (naming who bribed this round or last), or at once if the dealer pockets an inspector's bribe; 3 marked bribes pocketed also busts the dealer. The dealer wins at $300 pocketed. Hold B to see your role.

The dealer must hit until 17 and stands on every 17. Everyone starts with $500. Run out and you're done: bots leave the table, and you can start over.
