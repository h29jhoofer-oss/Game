HOW TO PLAY

Everyone plays against the dealer, not each other. Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over.

Number cards count their number, J Q K count 10, and an ace counts 1 or 11, whichever helps.

HIT takes a card. STAND keeps your hand. DOUBLE doubles your bet on your first two cards and takes exactly one more card.

SPLIT turns two cards of the same value into two hands, each with its own bet (up to 4 hands). Split aces get one card each, and a split hand of 21 isn't blackjack.

An ace plus a 10-value card is blackjack and pays 3 to 2. A win pays 1 to 1 and a tie (push) gives your bet back.

Cheater mode: one player is the dealer and holds a pistol. Secretly, one player is the cheater (sees the next 3 cards and the dealer's hidden card, wins at $1,000), one is the jester (wins if shot), and the rest are honest gamblers. The dealer gets one shot: hit the cheater and the house and gamblers win; hit the jester and the jester wins; hit a gambler and the cheater wins.

The dealer must hit until 17 and stands on every 17. Everyone starts with $500. Run out and you're done: bots leave the table, and you can start over.
