HOW TO PLAY

Pick a chip and click the table to bet on where the ball will land. The wheel has 0 to 36; zero is green.

A single number pays 35 to 1. A dozen (1-12, 13-24, 25-36) or a column (the 2:1 boxes) pays 2 to 1.

Red, black, odd, even, 1-18 and 19-36 pay 1 to 1. They all lose on zero.

Up to $200 a spin. Everyone starts with $500. Press SPIN when you're ready (online, the wheel spins once everyone's ready, or 20 seconds after the first bet).

The magnet: while the ball settles, you can nudge it one pocket left or right. It needs 5 spins to recharge, and everyone sees you use it.
