HOW TO PLAY

Everyone starts with 5 dice, rolled in secret under a cup. You see only your own.

Take turns bidding on how many dice of one face are on the whole table, counting everyone's dice. Ones are wild: they count as every face.

Each bid must go up: more dice, or the same number of a higher face.

Don't believe the last bid? Call LIAR! The cups come up and everyone counts. Fewer than bid: the bidder loses a die. Otherwise the caller does.

Think the bid is exactly right? Call SPOT ON. Right: you win back a die (up to 5). Wrong: you lose one.

Whoever loses a die starts the next round. Lose them all and you're out. The last player with dice wins.

Loaded die: once a game, you can secretly turn one of your dice to any face you like.
