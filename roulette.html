HOW TO PLAY

Everyone starts with $500. Each round, everyone still in antes $25 into the pot.

Then there's an auction: how many bullets will you put in an 8-chamber revolver? A random player opens with at least 1. In turn, raise to more bullets (up to 7) or pass. Pass and you're out of the bidding for that round.

When everyone else has passed, the top bidder faces the gun: their bullets go in, the cylinder spins and clicks shut, and it turns to point at them.

*Click*: they survive and take the whole pot. BANG: they're out of the game, and the pot stays on the table for the next round.

Can't pay the ante? You face a fully loaded gun.

The last one standing takes the pot and wins.

Cheater mode (3 to 6 players): one player is secretly the cheater, with 3 blanks a game. Before every shot there's a moment while the gun is loaded: the cheater can slip blanks in place of real bullets, into anyone's shot. Blanks look exactly like bullets, and a blank under the hammer just goes *click*. Hold B (or press and hold your own seat) to see your role. Once a game, anyone can call out the cheater between shots: right, and the cheater faces a fully loaded gun and you take all their chips; wrong, and you pay half your chips to the player you accused.
