DUNGEON BOSS

How to play You are the boss at the end of the level. Heroes run and jump through it to reach you.
Build: pick a tool and click the level. Spikes go on the ground; saws and turrets go anywhere, even over the lava pits. Click a trap of the same type again to upgrade it (max level 3).
Fight: A / D or arrows move, W jumps, Space swings your cleaver. You can't leave your lair.
Each dead hero gives coins and souls, but the next one is stronger, better geared, and better at spotting traps and dodging.
