DUNGEON BOSS

How to play You are the boss, waiting at the top of your castle tower. Heroes run and jump through the level to reach it.
Build: pick a tool and click the level. Spikes go on the ground; saws and turrets go anywhere, even over the lava pits. Click a trap of the same type again to upgrade it (max level 3).
Boss battle: when a hero gets through the tower door, the fight moves to the tower top. A / D or arrows move, W jumps, Space swings your cleaver, S slams the ground and sends out shockwaves.
Each dead hero gives coins and souls, but the next one is stronger, better geared, and better at spotting traps and dodging.
