How to play You are the boss, waiting at the top of your castle tower. Each wave, a group of heroes runs and jumps through the level to reach it. Heroes who make it through the door hide inside the tower. Once 5 are inside, they climb up and fight you all at once.
Build: pick a tool and click the level. Spikes go on the ground; saws and turrets go anywhere, even over the lava pits. Click a placed trap to open its menu and upgrade it (max level 3) or sell it for half what you paid; the Your traps list below does the same for every trap at once. The path is twice as long as the screen: scroll with A / D, the arrow keys, the mouse wheel, by moving the mouse to the screen edge, or by clicking the mini-map. Every hero you kill drops coins and exactly one soul, and kills unlock new traps: fire vents, crushers, bombs and frost totems.
Boss battle: when the tower fills up, the fight moves to the tower top. A / D or arrows move, W jumps, Space swings your cleaver. Special attacks, each with a cooldown: Q Cleaver Slash (dash through the heroes), S Ground Smash (hurls away everyone around you), R Grasp of the Damned (a pool of blood that holds heroes and drags the weak down to hell), F Hellfire (fireballs rain from the sky).
Phase two: the first time Vorgath falls in a fight, he transforms into the Devourer, with a second health bar and new attacks on the same keys: Space claw swipe, Q Soul Breath, S Cataclysm (a quake and erupting spikes), R Devour (chains drag heroes into his jaws; the weak are eaten and heal him), F Wailing Souls (homing skulls).
Heroes: knights charge in with swords. From wave 2, archers join: fast and fragile, they stop to shoot your minions and keep their distance on the tower. From wave 3, Iron Knights march in: slow and very tough, with a tower shield that blocks most arrow damage from the front. Crushers, fire and bombs work best on them.
From wave 4, a Champion leads the charge (one more every three waves): a hero boss, huge and slow, with far more health and a heavy greatsword. Champions grow tougher every wave. From wave 5, mages join: their bolts are weak, but they heal hurt heroes around them and empower one ally, who glows green and gains speed, strength and health. Kill the mage and the blessing fades.
Press M to mute the music and N to mute sound effects.
Each wave brings one more hero than the last, and they're stronger, better geared, and better at spotting traps and dodging.

Lore Vorgath, the Hollow King. Three hundred years ago, the kingdom of Ashfall was dying. Its king, Vorgath, climbed the highest tower of his castle and made a bargain with something that answered from the dark: his soul, in return for a tower that would never fall.
The tower still stands. Vorgath does too, after a fashion: a suit of black iron with nothing inside but hunger, and two red eyes that never close. He is bound to the tower, and the tower feeds on souls.
The Order of the Dawn has sworn to end him. Every year more of their squires, knights and paladins march on the tower. Every year the lava drinks some, the traps take others, and those who reach the top meet the Hollow King and his greatcleaver. Not one has come back down. Their souls are what keep him standing.
