How to play You are the boss, waiting at the top of your castle tower. Each wave, a group of heroes runs and jumps through the level to reach it. Heroes who make it through the door hide inside the tower. Once 5 are inside, they climb up and fight you all at once.
Modes: Story is the 25-wave tale of the tower and what lies under it. Sandbox has no story and no last wave: the heroes keep coming, a level stronger each wave, joined by eleven sandbox-only heroes (fliers, burrowers, a Siege Colossus every fifth wave from 10, and more). Pick Easy, Normal, Hard or Nightmare for it; each keeps its own best wave.
Build: pick a tool and click the level. Spikes go on the ground; saws and turrets go anywhere, even over the lava pits. Click a placed trap to open its tab on the right side of the screen: upgrade it (U) or sell it for half what you paid (X). The Thing Below: every soul the tower takes feeds something beneath it (dark souls and bargains most of all). As it grows the ground shakes, eyes open in the lava, a heartbeat thuds, and tendrils drag the living (even your monsters) down. After waves 4, 7, 10, 13, 16, 19 and 22 its Voice offers a bargain (1-3 to take one, Esc to refuse): power for a price. Wave 25 is the end: the Thing breaks through the tower top, and you fight it with the Marshal and her knights beside you. Its hide shrugs off blows; strike its great eye while it is open, and cut down the tendrils it sends up. Beat it and you choose the ending, unless you took 5 or more bargains, or the Marshal died.
Only one crusher fits in each column. More traps unlock with kills: grinders (5) are 3 tiles of gears set in the floor that chew and slow anyone crossing, skyhooks (8) fire harpoons that drag flying heroes out of the sky, tar pits (12) slow heroes and make fire hurt them more, tesla coils (20) shoot lightning that chains between heroes, boulder chutes (18) are 2 by 2 hoppers that roll boulders back down the path at heroes, bounding over the pits, and pendulums (25) swing a blade under their mount. Any trap can also be infused with a dark soul (Infuse in its tab, or I): +75% damage, 25% faster, 20% more reach. At level 3 you can specialize it down one of two paths (press 1 or 2). Each makes the trap better at one thing and worse at another, and changes how it looks: a crusher can become a fast Steam Piston that hits softer, or a slow Obsidian Slab that hits harder and sends out a shockwave. The Your traps list below does the same for every trap at once. The path is twice as long as the screen: scroll with A / D, the arrow keys, the mouse wheel, by moving the mouse to the screen edge, or by clicking the mini-map. Every hero you kill drops coins and exactly one soul, and kills unlock new traps: fire vents, crushers, bombs and frost totems.
Monsters: spend souls on skeletons and ogres, and as you kill more heroes, imps (spit fireballs from range), wraiths (fly over pits and walls and drain life), hellhounds (fast, pounce and set heroes burning) and bone golems (huge, and smash every hero around them). The Bone Serpent costs 2 dark souls and lives in a lava pit (one per pit): it can't be killed, swims slowly, and every 7 seconds or so it lunges up and swallows a hero jumping over its pit. Click its lava to feed it a dark soul. Champions are too big to swallow, so it bites them hard instead.
Dark souls: one hero in four drops a purple dark soul instead of a normal one. Spend them on Dark powers (below the toolbar) that you keep for every boss battle: Dark Mending (1), Doom Nova (2), Raise the Fallen (3) and the passive Unholy Might. Or click one of your monsters and infuse it with a dark soul (I): it turns black and violet, with 2.5x health, double damage, more speed, and it drains life from what it hits.
Boss battle: when the tower fills up, the fight moves to the tower top. A / D or arrows move, W jumps, Space swings your cleaver. Special attacks, each with a cooldown: Q Cleaver Slash (dash through the heroes), S Ground Smash (hurls away everyone around you), R Grasp of the Damned (a pool of blood that holds heroes and drags the weak down to hell), F Hellfire (fireballs rain from the sky).
Phase two: the first time Vorgath falls in a fight, he transforms into the Devourer, with a second health bar and new attacks on the same keys: Space claw swipe, Q Soul Breath, S Cataclysm (a quake and erupting spikes), R Devour (chains drag heroes into his jaws; the weak are eaten and heal him), F Wailing Souls (homing skulls).
Sandbox boss battles: Vorgath has twice the health in every form, and a fifth attack on E: Iron Tempest (spin and keep cutting, you can still walk) in his first form and Feast of Blood (a crimson wave that heals him for every hero hit) as the Devourer. When the Devourer falls he rises a third time as the Unmade, who hovers, can jump, and has five attacks: Q Void Lance (a beam across the tower top; heroes can jump it), S Eclipse (a black sun drops on every hero), R Soul Harvest (drags every hero in, then tears at them and heals him), F Doom Pillars (columns of black fire run out on both sides) and E Void Step (vanish and reappear behind the furthest hero with a blast).
Heroes: knights charge in with swords. From wave 2, archers join: fast and fragile, they stop to shoot your minions and keep their distance on the tower. From wave 3, Iron Knights march in: slow and very tough, with a tower shield that blocks most arrow damage from the front. Crushers, fire and bombs work best on them.
Later waves bring heroes immune to some traps: rogues (wave 6) never set off spikes or bombs, Flame Wardens (wave 7) ignore fire and fireballs, berserkers (wave 8) can't be slowed or frozen and rage when hurt, and sappers (wave 9) ignore crushers and saws and jam the traps they pass. Mix your traps!
From wave 4, a Champion leads the charge (one more every three waves): a hero boss, huge and slow, with far more health and a heavy greatsword. Champions grow tougher every wave. From wave 5, mages join: their bolts are weak, but they heal hurt heroes around them and empower one ally, who glows green and gains speed, strength and health. Kill the mage and the blessing fades.
Between waves, Vorgath, the Marshal and the Voice Below talk: press Space (or Enter, or click the box) to continue. Waves and boss battles are quiet.
From wave 10, Dawn Seraphs fly over the whole path: only arrow turrets, tesla coils and skyhooks can hit them, and a skyhook drags them out of the sky (into the lava, if they're over a pit). Heroes have double health on the path, and normal health in the boss battle.
Press M to mute the music and N to mute sound effects.
Each wave brings one more hero than the last, and they're stronger, better geared, and better at spotting traps and dodging.

Lore: the Chronicle of Ashfall Ashfall and the Grey Plague. Three hundred years ago, Ashfall was the richest kingdom in the north. Then the Grey Plague came up the river, and in one winter it took half the realm. Its king, Vorgath, watched his people die by the thousand.
The Bargain. On the longest night, Vorgath climbed the highest tower of his castle and called into the dark beneath it. Something answered. It offered a cure for the plague and a tower that would never fall, and it asked for his soul. He said yes. The plague ended by morning.
The Hollow King. The tower still stands. Vorgath does too, after a fashion: a suit of black iron with nothing inside but hunger, and two red eyes that never close. He is bound to the tower, and the tower feeds on souls.
Elowen. The king's only daughter fled the tower that same night, carrying nothing but her father's sword. Years later she founded a holy order and carved one oath into the Dawnstone: free my father. Her descendants have led the Order ever since.
The Order of the Dawn. Every year more of their squires, knights and paladins march on the tower: archers of the Ranger Lodge, Iron Knights sworn to the Iron Vow, mages of the Light, rogues, Flame Wardens forged in the Citadel's furnaces, berserkers from the frozen north, sappers who hate machines, and the great Champions who lead them all. The lava drinks some. The traps take others. Those who reach the top meet the Hollow King.
Seraphine Vane. The current High Marshal of the Order, and the last of Elowen's line. She speaks to the Hollow King between waves. She knows something he has forgotten: they share the same blood.
The Devourer. When Vorgath's iron body breaks, the thing that has been growing inside it tears free: the true form of the bargain, all jaws and hunger. Some in the Order whisper that it was never Vorgath at all, only the demon wearing him.
Dark souls. Most souls burn blue. A few, from heroes who died afraid or full of hate, burn black and violet. The tower treasures these above all: they feed dark powers, and they turn monsters and machines into something far worse.
The Thing Below. Whatever Vorgath bargained with never left. It has lived beneath the tower for three hundred years, tasting every soul he takes and growing fat on them. It whispers to him, and lately it offers deals. The Order's oldest chronicles say that one day it will grow too large for its cage and come up to take the throne of Ashfall for itself.
The Bone Serpent. An ancient thing of fused spines that sleeps in the lava under the castle. Vorgath can wake it with dark souls. It has never been killed, because nothing that has fought it has survived.
